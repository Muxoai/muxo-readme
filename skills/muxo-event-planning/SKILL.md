# Meeting & event coordination

Plan and run a meeting or small event end to end.

## The plan

**Phase 1 — Schedule**
1. Find open slots with `app.googlecalendar.*` (or `app.calendly.*`).
2. Book the event with a video link (`app.zoom.*` or `app.googlemeet.*`).

**Phase 2 — Invite & prepare**
3. Send invitations and the agenda with `app.gmail.*`.
4. Post a reminder and agenda to `app.slack.*`.

**Phase 3 — Run**
5. On the day, share notes/recap links; draft a follow-up in `app.gmail.*` or `app.notion.*`.

**Phase 4 — Follow up**
6. Send the recap and action items, and file them in `app.notion.*`/`app.linear.*`.

## Key actions

- Pick a time: `app.googlecalendar.*`, `app.calendly.*`
- Room & video: `app.zoom.*` / `app.googlemeet.*`
- Invite & follow up: `app.gmail.*`, `app.notion.*`, `app.slack.*`, `app.linear.*`

## Exit criteria

The event is scheduled, invites are out, and a recap with owners for each action item is saved.

## Guardrails

- Invites and calendar writes go out on your behalf: confirm attendees and timezone first.
- Editing a shared calendar may need confirmation.

## Defaults to remember

- Default calendar, meeting length, video tool, and the recap destination.

## Gotchas

- Timezones are the top failure: confirm the attendee's zone before booking.
- Never overbook; check availability first.
