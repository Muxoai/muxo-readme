# Employee onboarding

Bring a new hire on board end to end: paperwork, accounts, first-week calendar, and a tracked checklist.

## The plan

**Phase 1 — Paperwork**
1. Send the offer and welcome docs via `app.docusign.*` and `app.gmail.*`.
2. Create the employee record in the HR system or `app.airtable.*`.

**Phase 2 — Accounts & access**
3. Request tool access in `app.slack.*` (HR channel), `app.notion.*` (handbook), and any ticketing system.
4. Set up their docs folder in `app.googledrive.*`.

**Phase 3 — Schedule**
5. Book first-week meetings with `app.googlecalendar.*`, `app.zoom.*`, or `app.calendly.*`.
6. Send the agenda and links in `app.gmail.*`.

**Phase 4 — Track**
7. Maintain the onboarding checklist in `app.notion.*` or `app.airtable.*`, and nudge owners with `notifications`.

## Key actions

- Paperwork: `app.docusign.*`, `app.gmail.*`, `app.airtable.*`
- Accounts & docs: `app.slack.*`, `app.notion.*`, `app.googledrive.*`
- Schedule: `app.googlecalendar.*`, `app.zoom.*` / `app.calendly.*`; nudge with `notifications`

## Exit criteria

Paperwork is signed, accounts are requested, the first week is scheduled, and the checklist is live.

## Guardrails

- Personal data stays in the HR system/checklist, not chat.
- Email and scheduling are writes: confirm recipients and times before sending.

## Defaults to remember

- The onboarding checklist location, hiring manager, and default calendar.

## Gotchas

- Confirm the hire's name, email, and start date before sending anything.
- Schedule in the hire's timezone; ask when it's ambiguous.
