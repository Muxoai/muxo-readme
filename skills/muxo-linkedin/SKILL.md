# LinkedIn via Muxo

Connect LinkedIn with `connect`, then `search` for the exact action (e.g. "search linkedin people", "post linkedin update"), and `run` it.

## Common actions

- Search people and companies; read profiles and pages.
- Post a status update or article.

## Defaults to remember

- The target audience/industry for searches.
- Whether the assistant may post to the company page or only the person's profile.

## Rules & approvals

- Posting is a write: expect confirmation, and honor "ask before posting" rules.
- Respect profile-view and outreach limits; use Instant people tools for bulk data instead.

## Gotchas

- Confirm the exact profile/page before posting.
- For the full social flow, see `muxo-social-media`.
